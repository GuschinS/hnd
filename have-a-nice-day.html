Why this yacht

Drawn by Judel/Vrolijk

The German studio known for America's Cup and grand-prix racing yachts gave the 575 a clean hull without chines and a balanced helm.

Built in Germany for open water

Laminated at the Hanse yard in Greifswald and built to Germanischer Lloyd ocean-going standards.

Sailed by two

A self-tacking jib and electric winches mean tacking needs no extra crew, even on a 17-metre yacht.

A home on the water

A wide cockpit, a bathing platform with a tender garage, and about 2.10 m of headroom in a bright saloon.
